1. Scope

This policy applies to the Sim2Real website and the services used to register for and take part in the Sim2Real Robotics Competition, organised by RoboTech at the National Institute of Technology Karnataka, Surathkal (NITK).

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Account information: your email address, password in hashed form, and account verification status.
  • Participant and team information: profile details, institution, team membership, event registration details, and profile photograph if you provide one.
  • Competition information: submissions, quiz responses and results, payment status, and payment proof where a registration fee applies.
  • Communications: support queries, general enquiries, sponsorship enquiries, and our responses.
  • Technical information: information normally sent by your browser, such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, and security or diagnostic logs.

Please do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information in free-text fields or uploaded files.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and secure accounts, verify email addresses, and reset passwords;
  • register participants and teams and administer competition rounds;
  • review payments, submissions, quizzes, and competition eligibility;
  • send operational updates and respond to enquiries;
  • prevent misuse, investigate technical problems, and improve the website; and
  • meet applicable legal, safety, and institutional requirements.

4. How we share information

Authorised organisers and NITK personnel may access information where needed to operate the competition. We may also use service providers for hosting, email delivery, form protection, and other technical operations. Those providers process information for the relevant service.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect participants or the public, or to investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents. We do not sell personal information.

5. Storage and retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to run the competition, maintain records, resolve disputes, and satisfy legal or institutional requirements. Retention periods can differ by record type. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information associated with you. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate competition, security, legal, or record-keeping purposes. You may also choose not to provide optional information, although some features may then be unavailable.

7. External services and links

The website may link to external websites and may use services such as Google reCAPTCHA and embedded maps. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies. Sim2Real is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the competition or its services change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision. Material changes may also be communicated through the website or registered email address where appropriate.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact sim2real.robotechnitk@nitk.edu.in.
